## Facts
- Handshake works as documented for the 5L family: write both frames, pad acks with `02 50 03 00 00 59 F6 03`.
- Idle ping on this model is 6 bytes with a non-zero data byte: `02 51 00 01 08 03`.
- Status `0x02` is a start countdown (data byte counts 3, 2, 1).
- Checksum byte (second to last) is not understood; the app ignores it.
- Running telemetry was not captured: FFF1 went silent after the countdown. Next test enables every notify characteristic (2ACD, 2AD3, 2ADA, FEE1, plus 2AD9 indications) before starting the belt.
